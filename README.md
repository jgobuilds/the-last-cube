# The Last Cube

*A fun AI project between a father and son, learning AI together.*

**Play it: https://jgobuilds.github.io/the-last-cube/**

A blocky beat-'em-up. Your friend has been taken - fight through six levels,
beat six bosses, and save them. Sword up close, bow for the Sky Biters that
fly out of reach.

## How to play

| Keyboard | Tablet / phone | Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| W A S D | joystick (left thumb, anywhere) | Move |
| J | big sword button | Sword - tap for a three-hit combo |
| K | bow button | Hold to draw, let go to fire |
| L | roll button | Roll - you can't be hit mid-roll |
| L then J | roll, then sword | Dash attack |
| Space | star button | Turn into the Super Knight when the meter is full |
| Esc | pause button | Pause |

A game controller works too.

On a tablet, use **Add to Home Screen** in the browser's menu and the game
opens full screen, like an app.

## Things to find

- Smash wooden cubes for gems.
- Hit a shield three times to break it. Stars over a bad guy's head mean
  every hit does double damage.
- Pick a reward after every boss.
- Every level hides one golden cube. Find all six...

Made with [Godot](https://godotengine.org).
